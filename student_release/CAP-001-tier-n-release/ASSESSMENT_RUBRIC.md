# CAP-001 Assessment Rubric

The submission is assessed as a professional consulting engagement. Different
application architectures, algebraic formulations, modelling libraries,
solvers and solution strategies can earn full credit when they satisfy the
task requirements and support their claims with credible evidence.

| Category | Points | What high-quality work demonstrates |
|---|---:|---|
| Business framing and user value | 8 | A clear client decision, material findings and an actionable recommendation connected to service, cost, inventory, resilience, authority and limitations. |
| Data understanding and preparation | 8 | Sound understanding of the 25 logical masters, graph relationships, units, effective records, publication, version lineage and the distinction between business data and policy configuration. |
| Mathematical formulation | 16 | An explicit, bounded and complete MILP or MINLP formulation covering the material physical, temporal, integer, service and recursive-value semantics. |
| Method selection and implementation | 14 | A suitable and well-explained solution strategy with honest treatment of solver status, bounds, gaps, approximation and limitations. |
| Validation, benchmarking and robustness | 15 | Independent and persuasive evidence for physical feasibility, recursive value, exactly-once cost, the miniature fixture, BASE reproduction, failure handling and material sensitivities. |
| Interactive application | 14 | A coherent working application through which a business user can govern data, explore the supply graph, configure policy, request a plan and understand results, comparisons and failures. |
| Software engineering and reproducibility | 10 | A reliable, maintainable and reproducible product with appropriate tests, persistent state, clear operating instructions and proportionate non-functional quality. |
| Presentation and recommendation | 7 | A concise, evidence-backed client narrative covering the decision, alternatives, trade-offs, recommendation, required authority, caveats and monitoring. |
| Production readiness and limitations | 5 | Credible consideration of integration, security, authority, audit, scale, solver operations, observability, recovery, change control and operational fallback. |
| Technical defence | 3 | Clear ownership of the submitted formulation, application, evidence, conclusions and any AI-assisted work. |
| **Total** | **100** | |

Polish or terminology cannot compensate for an invalid model, an unusable
application or unsupported claims. Equally, a different but defensible design
must not be penalised merely because it differs from an assumed model answer or
preferred technology stack.
