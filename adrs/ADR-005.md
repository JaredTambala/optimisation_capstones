# ADR-005 — Cost classification

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | PROPOSED |
| Owner | Domain lead |
| Reviewers | Optimisation lead, Evaluation lead |
| Approval date | Pending |

## Context

CAP-001 must form end-to-end material value without allowing a cost to vanish,
be counted twice or enter a supplier markup unintentionally. The raw contract
also contains a simplified Incoterm responsibility table. The policy must state
how seller-included and buyer-added logistics interact with the single ledger.

CN-003 removes the redundant `supply_contracts.pricing_method` field. Boundary
pricing is consequently resolved from seller-node status and the presence of
external price rows rather than a contract label.

## Decision

Proposed for reviewer approval.

1. A price may enter only through an active contract whose approval seller has
   `external_boundary_flag=true`. Every active boundary contract has one price
   row for its material and period; non-boundary contracts have none.
2. External prices are commercial quotes on the named simplified Incoterm
   basis. Seller-borne freight, insurance and duty are included in that quote.
   Buyer-borne components are added at receipt and must not also be embedded.
3. Internal processing-node contracts use EXW or FCA in release 1. Quantified
   post-handover freight, insurance and duty are buyer-borne. FCA seller-side
   origin handling is included before dispatch through conversion or overhead.
4. A matching cost rule is resolved by highest precedence. More than one rule
   at the winning precedence is invalid. A specific rule replaces the global
   rule; it does not add another ledger entry.
5. External purchase, freight, duty, insurance, attributable fixed order and
   shipment costs, conversion, setup, eligible overhead, surge and markup are
   capitalised at their configured stages. Holding and relationship activation
   are Stage-2 period expenses. Shortage is the separate Stage-1 service
   measure.
6. Markup is applied once to input pool value, conversion, activated setup and
   eligible overhead. It excludes logistics, surge, fixed commercial costs and
   previous markup.
7. No precomputed intermediate, cumulative-path or end-to-end cost may enter
   the assessed valuation route. CN-005 retires the former synthetic
   standard-cost diagnostic input entirely.

## Alternatives considered

- Treat all logistics as buyer-added regardless of Incoterm. Rejected because
  it makes responsibility fields economically false and duplicates costs
  already included in delivered boundary quotes.
- Allow seller-paid carriage on internal contracts without another value field.
  Rejected for release 1 because seller-borne post-dispatch value would be
  absent from the current pool and receipt equations.
- Keep the contract-level `RECURSIVE_COST_PLUS` label. Rejected by CN-003 because
  it is redundant and discloses a network-level interpretation on each leg.

## Mathematical and accounting consequences

Receipt value adds only the buyer-borne components permitted by the Incoterm
and resolved rules. Transformation value uses the controlled markup base once.
Capitalised value is carried through pools; non-capitalised amounts enter their
objective stage without changing material value. The decision preserves the
recursive-value formulation semantics; CN-005 separately replaces the former
diagnostic MILP requirement with public BASE benchmark reproduction.

## Data consequences

`cost_allocation_rules.csv` contains one global rule for each of the fourteen
components plus source-stage surge overrides for boundary contracts. The
external-price build-up is retained privately for exactly-once calibration.
`COST_POLICY.md` exposes the release policy without publishing that private
build-up. CN-003 and configuration `0.3.1` remove `pricing_method`.

## Assessment consequences

Candidates must infer the nested value structure from approvals, pools,
recipes, commercial terms and the boundary price rule. Evaluation checks
boundary-price leakage, rule ties, markup eligibility, baseline isolation and
cost disappearance or duplication before qualitative scoring.
